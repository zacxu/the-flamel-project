# Generation findings display contract

`GenerationFindings.tsx` consumes the verified, immutable `generation.json`. Its shared route control is `scope` (`primary` or `supplementary`). The primary composition, selected model-energy pair and proposal formula selectors are local display controls; they never generate, relax, refit or reinterpret eligibility. Loading or failure shows `DataNotice`, without invented replacement values.

Primary MgO and TiO2 show both first-eight arms. Valid and valid-unique fractions divide the corresponding raw count by all eight emitted draws. The supplementary tab shows only the four additional MgO draws per arm, never a pooled yield. Selecting the tab preserves primary denominators. Native tab roles, arrow/Home/End keyboard navigation and table alternatives make the display accessible independently of bar colour.

Energy pairs have a separate selector and retain one selected structure per arm: MgO uses the primary-plus-top-up selection pool; TiO2 uses the primary first-eight pool. Energies are CHGNet eV per physical atom, with the saved P1-minus-nontrivial difference also expressed in meV per atom. The separate anatase/rutile reference comparison is within TiO2, not a DFT ground-state claim. All eleven selected records remain accessible in source method order, including zero-step optimizer outcomes, tolerance-qualified final SG and partial catalog matches. No cross-composition energy ordering is computed.

Each of six formulas exposes all eight emitted attempts. Reasons derive only from the saved exact-count and geometry-validity flags. A different exact cell is a representation/workload exclusion and does not establish physical instability. All twelve larger-cell new-formula diagnostics remain accessible as a subset of the same 48 attempts. Four prepared prototype substitutions remain a separate method baseline and do not replace the empty 0/48 transformer shortlist.

Catalog-absence tables retain the two historical snapshot identities and populations. Geometry matching explicitly covered 15/23 MgO and 34/44 TiO2 entries, using original-source cells of at most 30 sites. Historical absence or a partial unmatched geometry does not establish global novelty, generator-pretraining OOD or DFT stability. Structure buttons resolve through the actual W1 registry and select a recorded model stage or the entity's recorded default. Rows without a public saved geometry have no invented viewer destination.

Every table offers a browser-generated CSV for exactly its current rows. Pure helpers in `generation.ts` retain full source precision, RFC 4180 quoting, explicit units, empty unavailable/not-applicable values, study/release IDs, public source IDs and selection scope. Boolean false and recorded numeric zero remain values. CSV download URLs are revoked and immediately suppressed after their projection changes. `verify-generation.mjs` independently checks these helpers against hash-verified original frozen CSVs and verifies source identities and immutable baselines; actual browser/download QA belongs to the separate W5 acceptance record.
