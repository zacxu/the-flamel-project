# W5 independent scientific and requirements review

Reviewed on 22 October 2026 against the integrated implementation and the frozen original research records. This is a source/data review. Browser interaction, actual browser downloads, rendering, production delivery and physical-device observations belong to the separate root QA evidence and are not claimed here.

**Result: all eleven W5 requirements are implemented at source/data level. No unresolved scientific or implementation blocker was found.** The stationarity-column wording clarification identified during review has been applied and rechecked: the UI now says “Stationary at this setting” and explains stationarity at that job’s setting, consistently with the separate finite-check acceptance field.

## Independent data and export acceptance

The evidence verifier directly compared the shipped pure model with hash-bound original CSV/JSON sources, rather than deriving expected rows from the model output. [W5_EVIDENCE_VALIDATION.json](W5_EVIDENCE_VALIDATION.json) records **2,136 passing checks**, including all **630** role/phase/calculation/status combinations and all **36** reference system/status/elemental/spin combinations. Every filtered row preserves original ordering, complete field values, source identity, numeric precision and empty-null semantics. Zero-result filters retain CSV headers.

The review also read the companion acceptance evidence: [W5_GENERATION_VALIDATION.json](W5_GENERATION_VALIDATION.json) records **3,835** checks over 88 emitted draws, 48 new-formula attempts, twelve exclusions, six proposals, four prototypes, eleven model records and two selected energy pairs. [W5_UNCERTAINTY_VALIDATION.json](W5_UNCERTAINTY_VALIDATION.json) records **55,470** checks over thirty split/endpoint contexts, 300 retention selections, 300 saved decile rows and ten role rows. These are integrity/implementation checks, not new statistical evidence.

Source actions were inspected independently: all fourteen literal source IDs requested by the integrated findings/evidence controls resolve in the public provenance registry. The summary and methods source sets and all fourteen narrative-claim source sets also resolve. Raw record paths do not become browser links.

## Scientific distinctions checked

- Primary and supplementary denominators are separate. A selected one-per-arm energy pair is not a distribution or a symmetry-free training ablation.
- Single split/endpoint reliability, retention and deciles are distinct from the separately pooled Full-policy role panel. Its pooled counts and event-weighted radii are labelled accordingly.
- Initial is one shared model per split, not five duplicated policy-specific initial models. The thirty uncertainty contexts are five splits times six saved model endpoints.
- The final 3,418-row diagnostic population produces approximately equal deciles of 342 or 341 rows. Retention uses the saved ceiling rule; requested fraction is not substituted for actual retained fraction.
- SCF success does not establish stationarity, and setting-level stationarity does not establish final-geometry finite sensitivity acceptance. Process exit zero/JOB DONE can coexist with incomplete terminal SCF evidence.
- Three accepted geometries are distinct from two accepted original source-reference IDs and three accepted inventory memberships. Shared material IDs in overlapping systems are not deduplicated out of reference coverage.
- The roughly -0.005533 meV/atom MgO difference is below measured numerical sensitivity. Adjacent cutoff/k changes are measured finite sensitivity, not a rigorous total DFT error bound.
- CaO+2MgO provides a reference anchor only. No compatible CaMg2O3 candidate energy, reaction energy, complete hull or newly established stable material exists.
- CSV filters describe the exact displayed population. Their current model outputs preserve measured zeros, unavailable nulls, units, scoped claims and public source IDs. The claim export retains the original limitation text.

## Remaining verification boundaries

The DFT stationarity wording issue is resolved: the column is **“Stationary at this setting”**, matching the frozen `stationary_at_this_setting` field. Preliminary 50-Ry records are therefore not presented as checked at the final accepted 90-Ry setting. The correction does not alter acceptance flags or results.

Root browser QA, real downloaded-file roundtrips, responsive layout, failure/retry states and final release closure remain separate checks. This review does not waive them. W6 integrated presentation/accessibility/device work and W7 deployment remain later phases. Optional decile enrichment O3 is implemented from the existing saved rows; no additional scientific fitting, generation, inference, relaxation or QE job was performed.
