## Important scientific boundaries checked

- Historical labels are signed reference-hull distances in eV/atom. Near-stable retrieval uses the inclusive `≤0.05` threshold and reference-hull retrieval uses `≤0`. Fresh QE absolute energies and CHGNet predictions remain different energy references.
- Historical zero counts at zero queries are a derived display origin. Precision at zero queries, random scores and unavailable terminal measurements remain null; CSV blanks retain that meaning.
- The six composition proposals still have zero exact-cell-valid draws out of 48. The twelve larger-cell examples are an excluded subset of those attempts. Four prototype substitutions remain a different baseline method.
- The conditioning comparison uses the same pretrained CrystalFormer architecture. It does not establish the benefit of a symmetry-free versus symmetry-constrained trained architecture. Selected model energy pairs contain one chosen structure per arm.
- The accepted source inventory covers two of 161 primary memberships and one of 57 fallback memberships. The combined 218 count is memberships, not unique materials. Missing elemental/spin-sensitive entries block a complete hull.
- The roughly 900-second perturbation has a completed process marker but no accepted terminal SCF, energy, force or stress. Its null fields cannot become zero or an accepted geometry.
- Known MgO recovery, finite force/stress/sensitivity checks and a tiny unresolved absolute-energy difference do not establish a newly discovered stable material. Historical catalog absence does not establish current global novelty or generator-pretraining OOD status.

## Accessibility audit and correction

The immutable source palette gave Uncertainty strokes only 2.016:1 against the paper surface and Full strokes 2.740:1. The current table highlight also put the original Quality + Diversity marker below 3:1. A presentation-only helper now uses darker colours for those three policies, while preserving the original theme bytes, numerical data, explicit policy labels and distinct chart dash patterns. The integration gate independently calculates WCAG relative luminance and checks all five policy colours against five actual chart/panel/table surfaces (25 pairs, each at least 3:1), plus eight normal-text token/surface pairs (each at least 4.5:1). These measurements certify those specified colour pairs, not every possible rendered pixel.

Source inspection confirms native labelled selects/ranges, named scroll regions, table captions and row/column headers, keyboard chart activation with equivalent range/table controls, focus-visible rules, native modal focus containment and restoration, query-modal closure before opening source notes, explicit loading/error/retry states, non-colour status wording and reduced-motion gates. The separate final browser report records actual keyboard/focus, visual colour correction, mobile containment, viewer fallback and an emulated reduced-motion fixture. It does not claim system-preference changes, native hidden-page delivery, physical touchscreen testing or screen-reader certification.
