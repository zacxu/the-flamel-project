## Independent data and export verification

`scripts/verify-replay.mjs` passed **45,871 checks** in `reports/W3_REPLAY_VALIDATION.json`. It checks the pinned manifest and dataset SHA-256 identities and reads the original hash-bound selected journal, learning curves, endpoint CSV and metadata sources. It does not read the oracle or held-out evaluation-ID set.

- All 25 histories, 125 batches and 1,250 selected events match their original source identities, full-precision prediction/radius/diversity/score/target values and formula/system metadata. The selected population contains 720 distinct materials.
- Every intermediate cumulative near-stable count, reference-hull count and distinct-system count is independently reconstructed from query records and checked against the original curve CSV. All 25 endpoint records agree.
- All three metrics, split values and aggregate mean/sample SD are checked against original split outcomes. Missing and duplicated curve records produce explicit failures rather than fabricated zeros.
- For every batch, changing the raw outcomes and cumulative fields cannot change the pre-reveal projection. Both query CSV states are checked for exact scope, numerical precision, label visibility and source identity.
- Comparison downloads are checked for all 31 nonempty policy visibility sets, all six completed budgets and both aggregate/single-split modes. Every exported metric/SD is independently checked against the original curve CSV; zero precision and unavailable single-split SD are empty. The `point_origin` column explicitly identifies derived zero-query rows separately from recorded batch boundaries.
- CSV acceptance covers RFC 4180 commas, quotes, embedded CRLF, Unicode, signed numerical values, false booleans, missing values, non-finite rejection and spreadsheet-formula neutralization for string fields. Numeric negative energies retain their sign and full precision.
- The four frozen numerical-freeze/manifest/budget/event baseline hashes agree before and after these checks. No research experiment is performed by the verifier.

## Optional scope and presentation limits

**O1, the selected-query scatter or a genuine saved full-pool scatter, is deferred.** It is optional in both plans and is not needed for any core W3 exit condition. The completed interface exposes the selected-query table and recorded policy curves. It does not present selected points as a complete pre-reveal candidate pool. Any future full-pool enrichment requires its own archived inputs, explicit allowlist, payload-size review and truthful reveal scope.

The chart metric choice is temporary local display state. The requested replay identity fields are shareable in the hash route. Lines connect measured boundaries only; no tooltip or selectable point reports an unmeasured intermediate query count. Aggregate bands describe variation across overlapping historical split populations and do not establish statistical significance, predictive calibration, DFT stability or a new material discovery.
